Creators kept running "30-day" and "7-day" challenges for their followers on top of tools that weren't built for it — a WhatsApp group, a Google Form, a payment link, and a lot of manual follow-up. ChallengeMOB was one place to do all of it: sign up, pay, get the day's content, keep going.

## What happened

About 1,000 people joined a 7-day paid challenge on it. It was my first time putting something in front of strangers and asking them to pay for it — and finding out that they would.

## Where it is

Paused. It's the project I think about most when I think about the other half of building a company: not just making the thing, but getting it to people.
